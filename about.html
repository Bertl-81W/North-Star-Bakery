Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: to create a neighborhood bakery where people could find fresh, handmade food and a welcoming community atmosphere.

What started as a small local bakery has grown through support from families, commuters, and neighbors who value fresh bread, thoughtful baking, and personal service.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baking starts with good ingredients. We prioritize quality ingredients and work to source products thoughtfully whenever possible. Our approach focuses on freshness, consistency, and supporting the community around us.
